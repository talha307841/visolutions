# Product roadmap

## Phase one — implemented website

Thirteen marketing/legal/demo routes, reusable shell, responsive layouts, licensed stock video, browser object detection, local simulated events, architecture exploration, industry-specific content, validated Resend-ready form, metadata/social image/sitemap/robots, and persistent documentation. The current deliverable contains no operational surveillance service.

## Before public launch

Configure owned domain or Vercel URL, verified email sender, recipient inbox and public contact. Finalize business identity, website legal copy and retention practices. Perform real-device/accessibility checks, production Lighthouse measurements and a real delivery test. Consider durable rate limiting before running a lead campaign.

## Phase two — controlled pilot foundation

Define the first measurable use case with a prospective operator. Assess camera access/placement and obtain customer authorization. Prototype gateway stream management and selected-frame capture with reconnection, authentication and health monitoring. Benchmark hardware and bandwidth.

## Phase three — inference and events

Evaluate appropriate models on approved representative data. Build managed inference workers, queue/backpressure handling, rules, validation and deduplication. Establish performance measurements, evidence storage policy, event schema and human review procedures.

## Phase four — production operations

Add real tenant identity, role-based access, data isolation, secure gateway enrollment, authorized evidence access, audit logs, retention/deletion, notification reliability and operational monitoring. Validate backups, incident handling and security before broader deployment.

## Later assessment

Flexible onsite inference, ERP/barcode integration for warehouse verification, additional industries and international expansion depend on demonstrated value, privacy review and sustainable deployment economics. These are future options, not promised delivery dates.
